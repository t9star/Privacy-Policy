Privacy Policy for NoExif: Photo Location Remover

Application: NoExif: Photo Location Remover

Package: jp.tpp.t9s.noexif | Last updated: October 26, 2026

Privacy Commitment: NoExif is designed with a 100% offline, privacy-first architecture. It does not request internet network permissions and never transmits or collects your photos, videos, or personal data.

1. Overview

NoExif: Photo Location Remover ("we", "our", or "the App") is developed to protect your personal privacy by stripping sensitive GPS location tags and Exif metadata from your photos and videos before sharing. We respect your privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest standards of data security.

2. Core Privacy Principles & Offline Operation

  • 100% Offline by Design: The App does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. It has zero capability to connect to the internet, upload files, or communicate with remote servers.
  • Zero Data Collection & Tracking: We do NOT collect, monitor, store, or share your personal data, media content, or usage analytics.
  • Strictly Local On-Device Processing: When you select photos or videos, all metadata inspection and removal tasks are executed strictly within your device's local memory and temporary app cache. No media or metadata ever leaves your device.

3. Device Permissions & Data Access

The App requests only the permissions strictly necessary to process your media files locally on your device:

  • Photos & Videos Access (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES, READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / Storage Access): Required exclusively to allow you to select photos and videos from your local device library for metadata inspection and scrubbing. Access occurs solely upon your explicit user interaction.
  • Media Storage (Write/Save Access): Required to save the sanitized, metadata-free copies back to your local storage or gallery.
  • No Background Scanning: The App does not perform background scans of your photo library and accesses files only when you directly select them.

4. Google Play In-App Purchases

The App may offer optional premium features or licenses via Google Play's In-App Billing service (com.android.vending.BILLING):

  • All payment transactions are handled directly and securely by Google Play.
  • We do not receive, process, or store your credit card numbers, bank account information, or billing address.
  • For details on how Google processes transaction and payment data, please review the Google Privacy Policy.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

The App does not contain third-party advertising SDKs, tracking libraries, or analytics tools. We do not sell, rent, or trade any user information to third parties.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect functional improvements or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted to this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact Information
