1. Overview & 100% On-Device Processing

DocAnvil is an offline-first PDF utility application designed to keep your sensitive documents completely private. All PDF operations—including compression, merging, splitting/rotating, image-to-PDF conversion, handwritten electronic signatures, and AES-256 password encryption and decryption—are performed locally on your device.

Strict Local Execution Guarantee

Your document contents, passwords, and handwritten signatures are never transmitted, uploaded, or stored on developer servers or any external cloud servers.

2. Information Collection & Advertising

DocAnvil is built with a zero-collection architecture:

  • Zero Advertisements: DocAnvil contains zero third-party advertising SDKs or ad networks.
  • Zero Analytics or Tracking: The app includes zero analytics, crash-tracking, or behavioral telemetry SDKs.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, access, store, or share your personal files, names, email addresses, or document contents.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

DocAnvil offers an optional one-time Pro unlock (docanvil_pro_unlock). All purchases and license restorations are processed securely through Google Play Billing (com.android.vending.BILLING).

Your payment methods, credit card numbers, and billing details are handled solely by Google Play and are never shared with or accessible to the developer.

4. Device Permissions & System Pickers

DocAnvil adheres to the principle of least privilege. Instead of requesting broad storage read permissions across your entire device, DocAnvil relies on Android's built-in Storage Access Framework (Document Picker) and Photo Picker so the app can only access the specific files or images you explicitly select.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Any output PDFs and saved handwritten signatures remain solely in your device's private app storage (or the export location you choose). You remain in full control of your data and may delete files or signatures at any time within the app, or remove all local app data completely by uninstalling DocAnvil.
