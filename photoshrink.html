Offline Photo Shrink - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 8, 2026

1. Privacy Policy Overview

This privacy policy explains how our mobile application "Offline Photo Shrink" handles your personal information. We believe in complete transparency and maximum privacy, which is why all image compression and resizing features are designed to operate locally on your device without collecting or transferring your photos.

2. On-Device Image Processing & Zero Location Permission

All image compression and resizing tasks are processed 100% locally on your device, and temporary files are deleted from local cache as soon as the session ends. The Application does NOT request device GPS/location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION), and we do NOT collect, save, monitor, track, or transmit your images, files, EXIF metadata, or precise location to any developer server.

Furthermore, this application uses the Android native Photo Picker, which means we do not request or have access to your full device storage or gallery. We only access the specific photo you choose to compress.

3. Third-Party Services (Advertising & In-App Purchases)

While core image processing runs 100% locally on your device, the free version integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements and Google Play Billing for premium upgrades. To deliver ads, improve the geographical relevance of ads, measure ad performance, prevent fraud, and process transactions, these third-party SDKs may automatically collect and process advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / AAID), IP addresses and approximate location derived therefrom, device information (model, OS version, carrier, etc.), app interaction/diagnostic data, and purchase tokens. We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. If you have any questions, please contact us via the developer contact page on the Google Play Store.
