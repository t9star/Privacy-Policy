1. Overview & 100% On-Device Architecture

NotchTone: Brown Noise & Focus is an acoustic wellness and concentration support application designed to generate soothing brown noise and customizable notch-filtered tones directly on your device.

All audio waveforms are synthesized locally in real time using mathematical digital signal processing (DSP) algorithms. NotchTone does not stream or download pre-recorded audio from external servers and operates completely offline without requiring an active internet connection for sound synthesis.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Your privacy is our core principle. NotchTone is engineered to collect absolutely zero personal information:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any personal identification to use the app.
  • Zero Data Transmission: NotchTone does NOT collect, transmit, store, or sell your name, email address, physical location, IP address, device serial numbers, or usage history.
  • No Third-Party Trackers or Advertisements: The application contains no advertising SDKs, no behavioral trackers, and no third-party telemetry or marketing analytics libraries.

3. Locally Stored Data

All user preferences—including notch center frequencies, bandwidths, sound volumes, and custom presets—are stored exclusively in your local device storage (via Android Room Database and DataStore). This data remains strictly on your hardware, is never uploaded to any remote server, and is inaccessible to the developer.

4. Android App Permissions & Purpose

NotchTone requests only the essential system permissions required to deliver its core acoustic playback features:

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PLAYBACK: Enables the app to maintain smooth, uninterrupted background noise synthesis while your device screen is turned off or while multitasking in other applications.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS: Allows NotchTone to display ongoing media playback controls (play, pause, stop, sleep timer countdown) in the system notification shade and on the lock screen.
  • com.android.vending.BILLING: Handled strictly via Google Play's official in-app billing infrastructure to securely process one-time purchases and restore licenses for NotchTone Pro. The developer never receives or stores your credit card details or financial information.

Medical Disclaimer (Important)

NotchTone is designed solely as a general acoustic wellness, relaxation, and sound focus tool. NotchTone is NOT a medical device, nor is it a diagnostic or clinical therapy instrument.

It is NOT intended to diagnose, treat, cure, mitigate, or prevent tinnitus, hyperacusis, hearing loss, or any auditory or medical condition. If you experience chronic ear ringing, sudden hearing loss, dizziness, or ear pain, please consult a certified audiologist or ear, nose, and throat (ENT) physician immediately. Always listen at moderate, comfortable volumes to protect your hearing.
